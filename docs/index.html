Our first product

Cliff's HK Mahjong

A fully functional Hong Kong mahjong game, free, with no ads. Play offline, on your own, or with friends in person.

  • Free, with no ads

    The full game, with no purchases, subscriptions, or ads — nothing to unlock and nothing interrupting play.

  • Play entirely offline

    No account and no internet connection required. Play a full game against the AI anywhere, anytime.

  • Local multiplayer with friends

    Sit up to four players down at the same table using Google's Nearby Connections API — no server, no Wi-Fi network, no account, just devices in the same room.

  • Hong Kong scoring rules

    Faan values and chip conversion follow Hong Kong mahjong scoring, with table-specific rules like the minimum faan set once and remembered for future games.
